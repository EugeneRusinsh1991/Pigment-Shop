# Task 4: Automated Linting & Verification

## Goal
Establish automated rules and perform final visual checks to ensure custom typography overrides are not reintroduced into the codebase.

## Steps
1. Add ESLint / stylelint rules (or custom script) to block inline `fontSize`, `fontWeight`, `lineHeight`, and `fontFamily` props.
2. Verify overall UI appearance and layout integrity across major pages.
3. Mark typography standardization roadmap as completed.
